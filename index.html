About

I work on high-performance computing (HPC) and systems software. My current research asks, with real measurements, under what conditions Data Processing Units (DPUs) such as the NVIDIA BlueField-3 can reduce inter-node communication in distributed training through compression.

Research

Characterizing communication compression on DPUs for distributed training

Implemented a NetZIP-style lossless pipeline (byte grouping + LZ4, 16-core parallelism, chunk-level compression/transfer overlap) on the Arm cores of an NVIDIA BlueField-3 DPU, and compared it end-to-end against uncompressed transfer on the same RDMA path. Quantified that the uncompressed baseline shifts with RDMA-buffer page backing (4 KiB: 10.9 GB/s vs. THP: 24.6 GB/s), and that sender-side processing saturates at about 9 GB/s — so without dedicated hardware the compressed path does not beat uncompressed RDMA — yielding concrete design implications.
